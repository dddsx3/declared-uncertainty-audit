================
P3 - Declared versus measured uncertainty in Earth observation products
Target journal: International Journal of Remote Sensing (Taylor & Francis)
Typeset with elsarticle at 12 pt for a format-free initial submission.
Last verified: 2026-11-11

Everything in the manuscript is produced by the scripts below, in the order
given, from the two inherited CSV files. No number in the text, tables or
figures is typed by hand: tables and figures are generated from
data/p3_dataset.csv and data/stats.json, and the audit statistics are generated
from p3_dataset.csv by code/audit_claims.py, which also writes data/claims.json.

3. WHAT WAS REPAIRED, AND WHY IT MATTERED
-----------------------------------------
(a) C2. The four columns locator_declared / quote_declared / locator_actual /
    quote_actual were empty for all 50 rows. Under the paper's own definition of
    C2 that left the audit with zero auditable objects. They are now populated
    from data/p3_verification_log.csv, which already recorded, for each group it
    covers, the locator and the reading taken there. Coverage after repair:
    55 cells filled, 17 of the 17 admitted rows carry a locator (G18's declared
    row cites the same sentence and DOI as G17).

(b) same_product. The old text said the field "is one throughout"; the data give
    1 for the 17 admitted rows and empty for the other 33, which have no
    declaration to compare. The text now says exactly that.

(c) Ledger. recommended_use is now a function of the admission verdict and of
    parity, computed by audit_claims.recommend(). Before: 7 replace / 5 do-not-use
    / 5 retain, with four of the seven "replace" rows being rows the audit itself
    judged non-comparable. After: 6 replace / 2 retain (ratio exactly one) /
    3 statistic caveat / 3 support caveat / 3 do-not-use-as-sigma.

(d) G01. The old text called 0.0915 m a thickness error and reported a "stage
    contrast 9.0". Both 0.0089 and 0.0915 are freeboard-stage numbers
    (actual_metric = RMS of freeboard difference, stage_actual = freeboard), so
    their ratio 0.0973 is a statistic mismatch, not a stage contrast. Mechanism 2
    is rewritten around the quantity that is attributable: the same product at
    thickness stage gives 0.6163 against 0.701, ratio 0.879, so the ratio of the
    two ratios is 9.0 and the extreme under-report is a labelling effect.

(e) Family coverage. "Eight families have no assessable object" -> ten (the CSV
    gives 16 families, 6 with at least one assessable object).

(f) Stratification. The group sizes were 3/2/6 with medians 1.000/1.576/0.721.
    Recomputed: n = 3/3/8 with medians 1.000/1.429/0.721, and the bootstrap
    contrast between score<=1 (n=3) and score>=2 (n=11) is 0.821 (0.471, 1.361),
    not 0.644 (0.345, 1.109).

(g) Counts. "26 a footprint mismatch" -> 24 among the 36 non-assessable rows
    (32 among all 50); "33 have no locatable declared value" -> 33 have no
    declared value, since the locator is now present where a value exists.
